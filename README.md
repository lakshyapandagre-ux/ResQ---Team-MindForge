🌍 About ResQ

ResQ is a next-generation civic safety and emergency management platform designed to modernize how citizens interact with their cities during both normal and crisis situations.

The platform combines:

AI-powered assistance
real-time civic intelligence
emergency response systems
community-driven reporting
smart city services
disaster preparedness tools

into a unified modern platform.

ResQ empowers citizens, volunteers, and authorities to collaborate efficiently during emergencies and everyday civic issues.
